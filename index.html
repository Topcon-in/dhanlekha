👋 Welcome to DhanLekha — Important Data Safety Notice

This app helps you track your monthly fixed payment/budget, daily earnings (with source), and daily expenses 100% offline directly on your device.

⚠️ Important Backup Warning:
Your financial records are never uploaded to any external server. If you ever switch devices, factory-reset your phone, or clear browser data, please export your data beforehand using the "Save Backup File" button and store that file in a safe location (such as another device, PC, or personal cloud drive) so your multi-month and multi-year history remains safe.
